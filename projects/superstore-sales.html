Superstore Sales Analysis

write-up available

Overview

  • Performed end-to-end exploratory data analysis on 9,994 retail transactions spanning 4 years; uncovered that Tables and Bookcases were loss-making despite high sales volume.
  • Built 5 visualizations including monthly sales trend lines and regional bar charts; identified the West region as top revenue generator ($725K) and Technology as most profitable category ($145K profit).

Write-up

This project used the Sample Superstore dataset — 9,994 order line items across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology, spanning 2014–2017 — to find where the business actually makes and loses money.

Overall the business generated $2.30M in sales at a 12.5% profit margin ($286K profit). But that margin hides a split: Technology and Office Supplies both post healthy profits ($145K and $122K respectively), while Furniture as a category is barely profitable overall — dragged down almost entirely by two sub-categories. Tables lost $17.7K and Bookcases lost $3.5K, despite both selling in real volume — a classic case of a product line that looks fine on a sales report but is quietly losing money on every unit, likely due to discounting or high shipping/handling costs relative to price.

On the other end, Copiers ($55.6K profit), Phones ($44.5K), and Accessories ($41.9K) were the strongest sub-categories — all Technology, reinforcing that category as the one to double down on. Regionally, West led in total sales at $725K, followed closely by East at $679K, with Central and South trailing.
